🔒 Privacy & Device Access Commitments

We believe in complete transparency for engineering candidates:

  • Camera: Optional client-side computer-vision feedback runs on your local browser canvas. No frames, snapshots, or webcam streams are ever uploaded or saved.
  • AI Evaluation: When in Demo Mode, evaluations use deterministic local algorithms. When connected to a real provider, prompts contain only the question and candidate transcript text.
